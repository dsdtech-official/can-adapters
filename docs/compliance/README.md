## Scope and supporting reports

- The CE (EMC) attestation concerns Directive 2014/30/EU and lists
  EN 55032:2015+A11:2020+A1:2020 and EN 55035:2017+A11:2020.
  It references test report **AGC09162260901EE01**.
- The RoHS attestation concerns Directive 2011/65/EU and amendment
  (EU) 2015/863. It lists IEC 62321 test standards and references test report
  **AGC09162260901-001**.

These PDFs are laboratory attestations, not the full test reports or a
manufacturer-signed EU Declaration of Conformity. The referenced full reports
are not included here. The PDFs state that they should be used in conjunction
with the full reports and do not imply assessment of the whole production.
